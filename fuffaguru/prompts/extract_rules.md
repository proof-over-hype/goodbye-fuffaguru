# Prompt: extract the rules of a method from many transcripts

Paste several transcripts of lessons (with stamps and a source label for each). The goal is the
method *as taught*, layer by layer, with the source of every rule.

---

From the lessons below, write the trading method as a table of layers. For each rule give the
source (`label hh:mm:ss`) and a certainty tag:

- **said**: stated in words;
- **seen**: visible on the chart;
- **inferred**: reconstructed by you from several examples;
- **ours**: a threshold the trader never gives (leave it for the human to fix BEFORE any test).

Layers, in this order:
1. Context / higher timeframe (what decides the direction);
2. Place (zone, level, "order block", value area...): exact definition;
3. Waiting (what must happen before looking for an entry);
4. Trigger (the exact event that makes them enter);
5. Entry (market or limit, at candle close or intrabar);
6. Stop (where, and how far: in price, ticks or ATR);
7. Target (fixed R or a level);
8. Management (partials, breakeven, trailing);
9. Invalidation (what cancels the setup);
10. Filters (news, hours, instruments) - mark time-of-day rules separately;
11. Frequency and risk per trade, as declared.

Then list:
- every **vague rule** ("it depends", "you'll see it with experience"): these cannot be tested;
- every **contradiction** between lessons, with both sources;
- every place where they say the decisive part is **in a paid course or group**;
- the **thresholds the trader does not give** and that a test will need (one line each).

Do not evaluate the person. Do not suggest improvements. Do not fill gaps silently.
